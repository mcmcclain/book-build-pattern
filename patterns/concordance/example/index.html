Page maps

Each strip is one built PDF, drawn to scale across its full page count. Segments are chapters, sized by how many pages they occupy and colored by where the words came from. Hover or tab through a segment to read it. This is synthetic example data, not a real book — it exists so the page renders on a fresh clone.
